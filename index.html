I’m Jahid Hasan, a Ph.D. candidate in Computer and Information Technology at Purdue University. My research develops intelligent, data-centric systems that automate data discovery, preparation, and pipeline debugging. I work at the intersection of machine learning, database systems, and agentic AI, with an emphasis on fairness, reliability, robustness, and adaptability under real-world deployment conditions.

At Purdue’s Responsible Data Science Lab, I investigate how data quality, pipeline components, and changing data distributions affect machine learning systems after deployment. Rather than treating failures as model-only problems, I study end-to-end data science pipelines and develop targeted, interpretable approaches for identifying and repairing their weakest stages.

My recent work includes PipeLens, a causal framework for resolving malfunctioning data science pipelines; DataSift, a reinforcement learning-based framework for acquiring high-value training data; and Sentinel, a system for evaluating pipeline robustness under distribution shifts. I am also developing multi-agent methods for pipeline diagnosis and repair, along with predictive safety systems for outdoor extended reality.

I am advised by Dr. Romila Pradhan and contribute to NSF- and CASMI-funded research. My collaborations include researchers from AT&T Research, Cornell University, Université Paris Cité, and the Purdue Virtual Reality Lab.

I’m most excited by environments where research meets production—where ideas are tested against messy data, real users, and real constraints. I enjoy working across disciplines to turn responsible AI principles into systems that are practical, measurable, and scalable.

Feel free to explore my work, and don’t hesitate to reach out for collaborations, questions, or discussions!
