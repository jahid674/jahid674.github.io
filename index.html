About

I am Jahid Hasan, a Ph.D. candidate in Computer and Information Technology at Purdue University. I build reliable, data-centric AI systems for real-world use, combining machine learning, database systems, and agentic AI to automate data discovery, preparation, and pipeline debugging.

My work addresses a recurring industry challenge: models that perform well during development can fail when data, user behavior, or operating conditions change. I approach this as an end-to-end systems problem. By identifying issues in data acquisition, preprocessing, configuration, and deployment, I develop targeted solutions that improve performance without requiring expensive system-wide rebuilding.

I have built systems that diagnose and repair malfunctioning data pipelines, acquire high-value training data to improve accuracy and fairness, and identify fragile pipeline components under distribution shifts. My recent projects, PipeLens, DataSift, and Sentinel, have produced measurable improvements over established baselines and have been published at leading data-management venues.

I am advised by Dr. Romila Pradhan and contribute to NSF- and CASMI-funded research.

PipeLens

Identifying interventions for malfunctioning data science pipelines

PipeLens models a pipeline as a directed graph and tests causal interventions such as parameter changes, insertion or deletion of modules, and reordering of components.

Selective data expansion for fairer model performance
