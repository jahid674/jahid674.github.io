I’m Jahid Hasan, a Ph.D. candidate in Computer and Information Technology at Purdue University. I build reliable, data-centric AI systems for real-world use, combining machine learning, database systems, and agentic AI to automate data discovery, preparation, and pipeline debugging.

My work addresses a recurring industry challenge: models that perform well during development can fail when data, user behavior, or operating conditions change. I approach this as an end-to-end systems problem. By identifying issues in data acquisition, preprocessing, configuration, and deployment, I develop targeted solutions that improve performance without requiring expensive system-wide rebuilding.

I have built systems that diagnose and repair malfunctioning data pipelines, acquire high-value training data to improve accuracy and fairness, and identify fragile pipeline components under distribution shifts. My recent projects—PipeLens, DataSift, and Sentinel—have produced measurable improvements over established baselines and have been published at leading data-management venues. I am also developing multi-agent systems for automated pipeline debugging and retrieval-augmented applications that deliver grounded, context-aware recommendations.

I am advised by Dr. Romila Pradhan and contribute to NSF- and CASMI-funded research. My collaborations include researchers from AT&T Research, Cornell University, Université Paris Cité, and the Purdue Virtual Reality Lab.

I am especially interested in applied research and industry roles where I can translate new ideas into dependable products. I enjoy working with cross-functional teams, designing measurable experiments, and building scalable AI systems that remain effective under messy data, changing requirements, and real operational constraints.

Feel free to explore my work, and don’t hesitate to reach out for collaborations, questions, or discussions!
