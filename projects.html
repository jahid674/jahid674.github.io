Research Projects

  • Agentic AI for Data Science Pipeline Debugging
    Ongoing research

    • Developing a multi-agent framework that diagnoses and repairs data science pipelines affected by poor data quality, component failures, and suboptimal configurations.
    • Coordinating stage-level agents so that local diagnoses and repairs improve end-to-end pipeline reliability and performance.
  • PipeLens: Identifying Interventions for Resolving Malfunctioning Data Science Pipelines
    To appear @ Proceedings of the VLDB Endowment / VLDB 2026

    • Developed a causal debugging framework that identifies targeted interventions across database, machine learning, and retrieval-augmented generation pipelines.
    • Used data profiles and historical pipeline executions to recommend interpretable repairs, including parameter changes and component insertion, removal, or reordering.
    • Improved pipeline utility by 5–40% compared with Learn2Clean and BugDoc.
  • DataSift: Selective Data Expansion for Model Performance
    Published @ International Conference on Extending Database Technology (EDBT) 2026 [View Paper] [Watch Video]

    • Designed a reinforcement learning-based system that identifies and acquires high-value training samples through adaptive data valuation.
    • Combined multi-armed bandit selection with influence-based valuation to improve acquisition efficiency, predictive performance, and fairness.
    • Achieved 30–60% improvement over AutoData, SliceTuner, random sampling, and entropy-based selection.
  • Sentinel: Evaluating Pipeline Robustness Under Distributional Shifts
    Accepted @ IEEE International Conference on Data Engineering (ICDE) 2026
    Workshop version published @ ACM SIGMOD 2025 [View Paper] [Watch Video]

    • Developed component- and pipeline-level measures that explain how optimized machine learning pipelines degrade under deployment-time distribution shifts.
    • Enabled teams to identify vulnerable pipeline stages and make targeted reconfigurations without retraining the complete system.
  • FANSHIELD: Predictive Obstacle Assessment for Outdoor Extended Reality
    Submitted @ ACM CHI 2027

    • Developed an LSTM-based trajectory prediction system using headset, gaze, controller, and smart-eyewear motion signals.
    • Designed anticipatory safety mechanisms that predict user movement and reduce collision risks in immersive outdoor environments.
