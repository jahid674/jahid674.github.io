Better data. More dependable systems.

My work automates data-intensive stages of machine learning—from finding the right data to diagnosing and repairing the pipelines that use it.

Agentic AI for Data Science Pipeline Debugging

Developing a multi-agent framework that diagnoses and repairs pipelines affected by data-quality problems, component failures, and suboptimal configurations. Stage-level agents coordinate local decisions so repairs improve global reliability and performance.

PipeLens

Identifies targeted, interpretable interventions in malfunctioning database, ML, and retrieval-augmented generation pipelines. PipeLens uses data profiles and historical executions to recommend repairs, improving utility by 5–40% over Learn2Clean and BugDoc.

Jahid Hasan, Stanley Jiang, Tejendra Singh, Sainyam Galhotra, Romila Pradhan, and Divesh Srivastava

DataSift

A reinforcement-learning framework that identifies and acquires high-value training samples through adaptive data valuation. It improves acquisition efficiency, predictive performance, and fairness by 30–60% over competing approaches.
